Master Thesis

University research project · Germany

Designing a Web Based UAV Interface for Firefighting Dispatchers

Empirical Within Subject Study · N = 29

This thesis investigates how structured information retrieval can support firefighting dispatchers when monitoring UAV data during interruptions. A controlled laboratory experiment compared a structured retrieval interface with a baseline monitoring system. Results showed improved situational awareness during task re engagement without increasing cognitive workload.
